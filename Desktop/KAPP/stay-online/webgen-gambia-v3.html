How to Generate & Deploy Your Website
1
Fill in the Business tab
Enter your business name, sector, tagline, about text, contact info and social media links. The more detail you provide, the better the generated website will be.
2
Upload photos
Go to the Photos tab and upload your logo, favicon, and up to 9 business photos. Use high-quality images — hero shot, products, atmosphere, team, and location. Photos are embedded directly in the HTML.
3
Choose your sections
In the Sections tab, toggle which sections to include: Gallery, Reviews, FAQ, Pricing, Team, Blog, and more. Each section is built with real content from your business info.
4
Add custom content (optional)
The Content tab lets you add services, team members, and FAQs manually. This gives the AI specific content to work with instead of generating it from scratch.
5
Pick a style
In the Style tab, choose a template, color palette, font pairing, layout style, and animation. The AI follows your design choices to create a matching website.
Template → Colors → Fonts → Layout → Animation → Language
6
Generate the website
Click Generate Website. The AI creates a complete, single-file HTML website with all CSS and JavaScript inline. This takes 30-90 seconds depending on the AI model used. Preview appears on the right.
7
Review & download
Check the preview. If the client approves, click Client Akkoord to save it to the CRM, or click Download to get the HTML file. You can also Copy HTML to clipboard.
8
Deploy to hosting
Upload the HTML file to your hosting provider (e.g. Namecheap, Cloudflare Pages, or Netlify). The website is a single file — no server needed. Just upload and point your domain to it.
Download HTML → Upload to hosting → Connect domain → Live! 🚀
9
AI model tips
Claude API: best quality, needs API key. Worker: uses your Cloudflare Worker proxy, no key needed on client. Ollama: free, runs locally, needs a capable model (8B+ recommended).
