How to Use Social Media for Your Gambian Business
1
Create your business accounts
Set up a Facebook Business Page and Instagram Business Account. Use your business name, logo, and contact info. Link your Instagram to your Facebook page for cross-posting.
2
Fill in your business details
In the Post tab, enter your business name, WhatsApp number, and location. These details are included in every generated post so customers can reach you.
3
Choose your post type & write what to post about
Select a post type (product, promotion, announcement, etc.) and describe what you want to post about. The more detail you give, the better the AI-generated post will be.
Example: "New menu item: Domoda with fresh vegetables, D75 per plate"
4
Select platforms & language
Choose which platforms to generate for: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp. Each platform gets a tailored post. Pick your language — English, Wolof, Mandinka, or French.
5
Generate & copy your posts
Click Generate Posts with AI. The tool creates optimized posts for each platform with hashtags. Click Copy to paste directly into Facebook, Instagram, or WhatsApp.
6
Schedule your posts
Use the Schedule tab to plan posts ahead. Pick a date on the calendar, add your post content, and set the time. Consistent posting (3-5 times per week) builds your audience.
7
Use templates for quick posts
The Templates tab has ready-made post formats for common situations — promotions, new products, testimonials, and more. Click a template to auto-fill the post form.
8
Best times to post in The Gambia
Facebook: 12pm-2pm & 7pm-9pm · Instagram: 11am-1pm & 6pm-8pm · WhatsApp Status: 8am-9am & 8pm-10pm. Post when your customers are most active!
