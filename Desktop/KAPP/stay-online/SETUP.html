1
Create a free Cloudflare account
Free · 5 min
▶
Cloudflare is where you host your website and run the backend. It's completely free for what you need.
1
Go to cloudflare.com and click "Sign Up". Use your email address.
2
Verify your email address.
3
Skip the "Add a site" step — click "Maybe later".
Open Cloudflare →
Tip: Write down your email and password. You'll use this account for all your clients' websites too.
2
Deploy the Worker (backend)
Free · 15 min
▶
The Worker is the backend that hides your API key and stores your CRM data in the cloud. You only do this once.
1
In Cloudflare dashboard, click Workers & Pages in the left menu.
2
Click "Create" → "Create Worker".
3
Give it the name: webgen-gambia. Click "Deploy".
4
Click "Edit code". Delete all existing code. Copy and paste the content of worker.js (from your folder). Click "Deploy".
https://webgen-gambia.YOUR-NAME.workers.dev
Copy
5
Go to your Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets. Add:
ANTHROPIC_API_KEY = your sk-ant-... key
ADMIN_PASSWORD = your CRM password (e.g. KOK2025)
6
Go to Workers & Pages → KV. Create a namespace called CRM_DATA. Copy the ID. Go back to your Worker → Settings → Bindings → Add KV binding: name = CRM_DATA, namespace = the one you just created.
7
Copy your Worker URL (looks like webgen-gambia.yourname.workers.dev) and paste it at the top of this page.
Important: Never share your ANTHROPIC_API_KEY with anyone. It gives access to your account and costs you money if misused.
3
Put your tools online (Cloudflare Pages)
Free · 10 min
▶
Upload your WebGen folder to Cloudflare Pages. Your tools are then accessible from any phone or computer.
1
In Cloudflare dashboard → Workers & Pages → Create → Pages → Upload assets.
2
Give your project a name: webgen-gambia.
3
Drag your entire WebGen Gambia folder into the upload area. Click "Deploy site".
4
Your site is now live at webgen-gambia.pages.dev. Bookmark this URL!
5
To update (when you add new files): go back to Pages → your project → click "Create new deployment" and upload again.
Result: You can now open your tools on your phone, your laptop, your client's computer — anywhere with internet. Open yourname.pages.dev/index.html
4
Connect the tools to your Worker
Free · 5 min
▶
Enter your Worker URL below and click "Save & Update All Tools". This automatically configures all tools to use your Worker instead of a direct API key.
⚡ Quick Connect
What changes: The tools will no longer need an API key field — they use your Worker automatically. The CRM data syncs to the cloud so you can access it from any device.
5
Connect your Namecheap domain (optional)
~D600/year
▶
Instead of yourname.pages.dev, use a professional domain like webgengambia.com or kokwebgen.gm.
1
Buy a domain on namecheap.com (e.g. webgengambia.com ~$10/year or kokweb.gm from gamdns.gm)
2
In Namecheap → Domain List → Manage → Nameservers → select Custom DNS. Enter Cloudflare nameservers:
nina.ns.cloudflare.com otto.ns.cloudflare.com
3
In Cloudflare → Add a site → enter your domain. Follow the setup wizard.
4
In Pages → your project → Custom domains → add your domain. Done!
