How to Set Up Google Business Profile
1
Create or claim your Google Business Profile
Go to business.google.com and sign in with a Google account. Search for your business — if it already appears on Google Maps, claim it. If not, click "Add your business" and follow the steps.
2
Verify your business
Google needs to verify you own the business. For most Gambian businesses, verification is by phone call or SMS. Sometimes a postcard is sent. Verification can take 1-7 days.
Phone/SMS → Enter code → Verified ✓
3
Fill in your business info using this tool
Enter your business details in the Business tab — name, category, address, phone, description, USPs, opening hours, and attributes. Then click Generate to get optimized content.
4
Copy the SEO description to your profile
The generated business description is optimized for Google Search with local keywords. Copy it and paste it into your Google Business Profile under Info → Business description.
5
Add Q&A pairs
Go to your Google listing and add the generated Q&A pairs as questions + answers. This helps customers find info quickly and improves your ranking. You can ask and answer your own questions!
6
Prepare review responses
Save the generated review response templates. When customers leave reviews, respond quickly using these templates — edit them to be personal. Responding to reviews boosts your visibility on Google.
7
Publish Google Posts regularly
Use the generated Google Posts to share updates, offers, and events. Go to your Google Business dashboard → Posts → Create post. Post at least once a week to stay active and visible.
8
Add photos & keep your profile updated
Upload at least 5 high-quality photos — your storefront, interior, products, and team. Businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests. Update your hours for holidays and special events.
